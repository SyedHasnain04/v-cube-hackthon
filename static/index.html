Library policy assistant

Ask anything about the library.

Membership rules, borrowing limits, overdue fines and digital access. Every answer is drawn from the library's own documentation, with the passages it used shown alongside.

How answers are grounded

General-purpose models tend to invent circulation limits and fees. Athenaeum first searches the policy documents, then writes an answer only from what it found.

  1. 01

    Document ingestion

    Policies are split into chunks that keep their section headings.

  2. 02

    Vector retrieval

    all-MiniLM-L6-v2 embeddings and a FAISS cosine-similarity index find the matching clauses.

  3. 03

    Guarded synthesis

    Gemini 2.5 Flash answers from the retrieved text and returns a match score per source.
