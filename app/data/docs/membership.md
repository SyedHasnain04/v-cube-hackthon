# Library Membership Guidelines
<!-- Sample documentation for Athenaeum RAG demonstration -->

## Eligibility and Registration
All individuals residing, working, or attending school within the municipal district are eligible for a free Athenaeum public library card. To register for a standard card, applicants must present a valid government-issued photo ID (such as a driver's license or passport) alongside current proof of residency (such as a utility bill or lease agreement issued within the past 60 days). Registration forms may be initiated online or completed in person at any library service desk.

## Membership Categories
- **Resident Card**: Full borrowing and digital access privileges for all local residents. Cards remain active for two years and can be renewed with verified proof of address.
- **Youth & Student Cards**: Available to minors under 18 years of age. A parent or legal guardian must co-sign the registration and accepts responsibility for borrowed materials.
- **Non-Resident Visitor Card**: Individuals residing outside the district service zone can obtain a non-resident card for an annual fee of $35, granting physical borrowing and select on-site digital database access.
- **Educator Pass**: Teachers, homeschool instructors, and educational facilitators are eligible for extended loan terms and higher item volume limits upon presenting educational credentials.

## Account Management and Card Replacement
Library cards are non-transferable and must be presented (or scanned via the library mobile barcode) to check out items. If a card is lost or stolen, members must notify the library circulation department immediately to freeze the account against unauthorized use. A replacement card may be issued for a administrative fee of $2.
