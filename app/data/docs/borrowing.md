# Library Borrowing and Circulation Policies
<!-- Sample documentation for Athenaeum RAG demonstration -->

## Loan Limits and Item Capacities
Standard adult library cardholders in good standing may borrow up to 25 items simultaneously across all physical formats. Specific category sub-limits include:
- Up to 15 print books and paperbacks
- Up to 5 audiobooks on CD or Playaway formats
- Up to 5 feature film or documentary DVDs/Blu-rays
- Up to 2 technology equipment kits or museum family passes

Youth and student cardholders have a total borrowing capacity of 10 physical items concurrently.

## Loan Periods
- **General Collection Books**: 21 days
- **New Release Fiction & Bestsellers**: 14 days
- **Periodicals and Magazines**: 14 days (current display issues remain in-library only)
- **Audiobooks & Music CDs**: 21 days
- **DVDs and Blu-rays**: 7 days
- **Equipment and Kits**: 7 days

## Renewals and Hold Requests
Eligible items may be renewed up to three times, provided there are no active hold reservations placed by other patrons. Automatic renewals occur 48 hours prior to the due date for items that qualify. Renewals can also be processed online through the patron portal, by telephone, or via self-checkout kiosks. Patrons can place holds on up to 10 circulating items at any time; items on hold will be reserved for 7 calendar days after patron notification.
