# Overdue Policies and Digital Resources
<!-- Sample documentation for Athenaeum RAG demonstration -->

## Overdue Guidelines and Fine-Free Policy
The Athenaeum operates under a fine-free circulation model for all standard circulating books and audiovisual materials. Courtesy reminders are transmitted via email or SMS at 3 days before the due date, and formal overdue notices are dispatched at 7 and 14 days overdue. If an item is more than 30 days overdue, it is declared lost, and an automated replacement fee equal to the publisher's list price plus a $5 cataloging fee is charged. Returning the overdue item in undamaged condition completely waives the replacement fee.

## E-Books, Audiobooks, and Streaming Access
All active cardholders have 24/7 access to digital library platforms:
- **Libby / OverDrive**: Borrow up to 10 e-books and digital audiobooks at a time with loan durations of 7, 14, or 21 days. Digital items return automatically on their due date, precluding any late fees.
- **Hoopla Digital**: Instant streaming access to movies, television series, music albums, and comics. Members receive 5 instant checkout credits per calendar month.
- **Kanopy**: Stream high-quality independent cinema, foreign films, and educational documentaries with 15 monthly play credits.

## Research Databases and Digital Logins
Access academic journals and premium learning databases including JSTOR, EBSCOhost, Gale Literature, and Morningstar Financial directly through the library website. To authenticate, patrons simply input their 14-digit library card barcode and 4-digit PIN (defaulting to the last four digits of their primary registered phone number).
